# F0CommunityPostsList

The Communities widget's content as a **list**: the latest posts as full-width
rows — a cover beside each post's title and the opening lines of its body, the
poster under them — divided by a rule that reaches the card's own border.

It is the other shape of `F0CommunityPostsCarousel`, and the two take the same
posts: `CommunityPostListItem` extends the tile's own `CommunityPostSummary`, so
switching between them is a change of component and not of data.

## Anatomy

A row is its cover, its words and its byline. The list opens with three of them
and puts the rest behind "View more"; the toggle says how many are still hidden
and turns into "View less" once they are out.

An **event** post is drawn as the event card the Communities feed itself draws —

previews a line more of the post now that the picture is not taking one.

said were coming — so the card is already the height it will be.

## Guidelines

### Design best practices

- **Give it the main column.** A cover beside a title and two lines of body needs
  the room to be beside anything. In a rail the rows stack into columns and each
  post costs a screenful.
- **Let the row be the target.** The whole row opens the post; there is no "Read
  more" and nothing else in it is clickable, which is also why the post's own
  links are not live in the preview.
- **Keep the counters in words.** `counters` takes strings — "128 visits", "4
  comments" — because the plural, the thousands separator and the noun are the
  app's locale and not this component's.

### When to use

Reach for the list when Communities is **one card among several** on a Home and
the reader is skimming: three posts visible at once, the rest one press away, and
nothing to operate to see the second one.

### When not to use

- **When the posts are what the card is for.** If Communities is the page rather
  than a widget on it, `F0CommunityPostsCarousel` previews more of each post and
  is the better read.
- **In a rail or any narrow column.** It still works, but a stacked cover and
  three rows fill a rail; a `list` slot's plain rows say more per pixel there.
- **For a feed you page through.** This holds the posts it is given and expands
  to all of them. A feed that arrives a page at a time belongs in the carousel,
  which asks for the next page itself.

## Accessibility

- **One target per row, named by the post.** The row is opened through a single
  anchor (or button, for a post with no `href`) stretched over it, so a screen
  reader hears the post's title once rather than a link per part of the row. An
  event row's card holds a title, a time and a place, so its target is named with
  the title alone.
- **Covers are decorative.** The picture carries `role="presentation"`: the title
  beside it already names the post, and an `alt` repeating it would read the post
  twice.
- **Axe blocks on these stories** (`a11y: { test: "error" }`) rather than warning
  — a stretched hit area is exactly the shape that loses its accessible name
  without anyone noticing.
